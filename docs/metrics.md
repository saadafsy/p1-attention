# Metrics: accuracy and utilization

Generated by `python scripts/accuracy_report.py --trials 10` (seed 1). Cycle
counts are the cocotb simulation values in tb/attention_top/cycles.txt; no
clock is involved, so nothing here is an Fmax or wall-clock claim.

Accuracy: the bit-exact fixed-point model (what the RTL is checked against)
versus an IEEE double reference on the dequantized inputs. Error is in output
LSBs (1 LSB = 2^-6). Roughly 0.25 LSB mean error is the floor set by rounding
the output to an integer LSB, so the softmax approximation adds little on top.

| N | input range | trials | max abs err (LSB) | mean abs err (LSB) | cycles | MAC/cycle |
|---|-------------|--------|-------------------|--------------------|--------|-----------|
| 4 | full int8 | 10 | 0.756 | 0.2526 | 173 | 3.0 |
| 4 | small +-32 | 10 | 0.557 | 0.2587 | 173 | 3.0 |
| 16 | full int8 | 10 | 0.805 | 0.2565 | 881 | 9.3 |
| 16 | small +-32 | 10 | 0.583 | 0.2550 | 881 | 9.3 |
| 64 | full int8 | 10 | 0.718 | 0.2534 | 6593 | 19.9 |
| 64 | small +-32 | 10 | 0.535 | 0.2470 | 6593 | 19.9 |

MAC/cycle counts 2*N^2*D MACs (Q.K^T plus PV). The engine has 48 multipliers
(docs/benchmark.md), so 19.9 MAC/cycle at N = 64 is 41% of all 48, or 62% of
the 32 that perform golden-model MACs. Run cycles exclude loading Q/K/V.

Limits of this table: the reference is float64, not fp32; inputs are
uniform random, not real attention activations; 10 trials per row.
